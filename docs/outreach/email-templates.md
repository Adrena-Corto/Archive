## Personalization Notes

Before sending, customize each email with:

1. **Specific reference to their work** — Don't just say "I admire your collection." Mention:
   - A specific piece they donated
   - An article or interview about them
   - A shared interest (Byzantine coins, jewelry, seals)

2. **Relevant piece from your collection** — "I recently acquired a [type] that reminded me of [their interest]"

3. **Mutual connection if any** — Did you meet someone at HKINF? Attended same auction?

## Timing Tips

- **Best days:** Tuesday-Thursday
- **Best time:** 9-11am HK time
- **Follow-up:** Wait 7-10 days, then one gentle follow-up
- **Don't batch:** Send 2-3 per week max to maintain quality

---

## What NOT to say

- ❌ "I'm looking for investors" (sounds transactional)
- ❌ "Would you like to fund..." (too direct for first contact)
- ❌ Long lists of what you need
- ❌ Attachments on first email (spam filters)

## What TO convey

- ✅ You're already doing the work (not asking permission)
- ✅ You have substance (1,000+ pieces, live website)
- ✅ You're offering connection, not asking for money
- ✅ You're bringing something new (tech angle)
- ✅ You're rooted in HK (family, 7 years)
