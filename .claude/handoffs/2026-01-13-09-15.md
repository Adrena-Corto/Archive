# Session Handoff - 2026-01-13 09:15

## Accomplished

### Timeline UX Improvements
- **Smoother axis label transitions**: Replaced discrete zoom-level-based tick intervals with dynamic calculation based on viewport span. Labels now transition gradually instead of jumping abruptly between 100-year and 1000-year intervals.

- **Date range indicator on hover**: When hovering items with uncertain dates (yearStart !== yearEnd), a dimmed horizontal bar appears showing the full date range with small diamond endpoints. Works for both single items and clustered thumbnails.

- **Cluster thumbnail interaction overhaul**:
  - Hovering individual thumbnails in a cluster now shows that item's tooltip (name, era, image) instead of generic "X items - Click to expand"
  - Clicking thumbnails navigates directly to item page
  - Clicking the pulsing diamond or "X items" label zooms into the cluster

- **Visual hints for cluster expand**:
  - Cluster diamonds now pulse subtly (±15% scale) to indicate clickability
  - "X items" label now shows "X items ⤢" with expand arrow icon

- **Reduced thumbnail motion**: Increased lerp speed from 0.08 to 0.25 for tighter scroll/zoom following

- **Fixed single item labels**: Labels now show when thumbnails are visible, regardless of zoom level (previously required zoom level >= 2)

## Key Decisions

- **Dynamic tick intervals over zoom levels**: Chose to calculate intervals based on `yearsVisible / targetLabelCount` and snap to nice numbers rather than hard-coded zoom level thresholds. This provides smoother transitions.

- **Thumbnail hover shows item tooltip**: Decided individual thumbnail hover should show item details rather than cluster info. The "click to expand" action is now implicit (diamond/label) rather than explicit on every thumbnail.

- **Lerp speed 0.25**: Balance between responsive feel and some smoothing. Could go higher (0.4-0.5) for even snappier response if needed.

- **Label visibility tied to thumbnailsVisible**: When thumbnails are shown (<=12 items visible), always show labels for non-clustered items regardless of zoom level.
