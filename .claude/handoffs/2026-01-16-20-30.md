## Key Decisions

1. **Touch magnifier positioned above finger** - So user can see the zoomed content without their finger blocking it. Offset is 40px above touch point.

2. **Swipe thresholds** - Chose conservative values (50px min, 300ms max, <100px vertical) to avoid accidental triggers while scrolling.

3. **Parallax disabled entirely on mobile** - Rather than trying to make it work on touch devices, disabled it completely to avoid jank and battery drain.

4. **Larger mobile menu button** - Increased from 48x48 to 56x56 pixels with negative margin to maintain visual alignment while improving tap target.

5. **User selection disabled globally with opt-in** - Rather than opt-out per element, disabled globally with `.prose` and `.selectable` classes for content that needs selection.
