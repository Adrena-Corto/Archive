## Outreach Strategy

### For HNW Collectors (Tier 1-2):
1. **Warm introduction preferred** — Ask numismatic contacts (Ma Tak Wo, auction house reps) for intros
2. **If cold outreach**: Short, personal email referencing their collection and a specific piece in yours that connects
3. **Offer value first**: "I'd welcome your expertise on [specific item]" rather than asking for money

### For Foundations (Tier 3):
1. **Apply formally** — Follow grant application processes
2. **Frame as cultural preservation** — Emphasize Hong Kong's role as heritage hub
3. **Start small** — Documentation or digitization project, not "build me a museum"

### For Museums (Tier 4):
1. **Offer loans** — "We'd be honored to lend [piece] for your upcoming exhibition"
2. **Propose research** — "Would your curatorial team be interested in examining our Mesopotamian seals?"
